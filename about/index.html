About Fallen Firms

Fallen Firms tells how American companies rose and fell, from the 10-Ks and court filings: Toys R Us, Sears, WeWork, Kodak, Enron, with every number sourced.

Who makes this

Fallen Firms is a Vision Media channel on YouTube. The research and the scripts are ours. The narrator is a text-to-speech voice; every video description says as much.

How we work

Each claim traces to a document we name: a 10-K, a court docket, a bankruptcy filing, a newspaper of record. Those documents are listed in the video description and at the foot of every article. Images are public domain, openly licensed or our own, and the credits page names them per video.
